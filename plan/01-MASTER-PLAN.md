## 1. Business model and economics

**What we are:** an organiser-coordinator, launching with inquiries and provisional estimates. We confirm scope, final price and availability manually by WhatsApp; bookings stay disabled until Gate C. Operator: Baby Shower Paraguay, sitio operado desde Asunción, with WhatsApp and SITE.email; no personal name unless Q16 is answered.

**Operating model (planned, confirm in Q1 before Gate C):** contractual reseller. Once bookings are enabled, we collect the agreed price, issue the customer document and buy fulfilment from suppliers. Inquiry mode does not request deposits.

**Planning estimates, not firm prices or confirmed supplier rates. Unit economics per Baby Shower Combo (30 pax), fully loaded (Codex stress-tested):**

| Line | Base case PYG | Stress case PYG |
|---|---|---|
| Retail price | 1.850.000 | 1.850.000 |
| Supplier decor + mesa dulce + bocaditos | 1.100.000 | 1.400.000 |
| Transport / coordination time (own) | 150.000 | 150.000 |
| Payment fees (card 3.5%, when used) | 65.000 | 65.000 |
| Incident reserve | 50.000 | 50.000 |
| Contribution before ads | 485.000 | 185.000 |
| Ads allowance per closed deal (cap) | 150.000 | 150.000 |
| Contribution after ads | 335.000 | 35.000 |

Rules that follow: (a) a written supplier rate card is a Gate C booking requirement; (b) every quote passes a margin floor of 300.000 PYG contribution before ads or it is not sent; (c) decor-only tier exists so a lower budget buys fewer deliverables, never the same event at a loss; (d) Ads max cost per qualified lead 30.000 PYG at a 20% lead-to-deposit rate.

Break-even on fixed costs (domain ~153k, hosting already paid, WhatsApp Business free): the first deal. Working capital: seña 50% of 1.850.000 = 925.000 covers a supplier deposit of up to 70% of 1.250.000 (875.000); if the supplier asks more, Anton funds the gap.

**Ladder (per customer):**
1. Revelación de Género: Kit Sorpresa estimated Gs. 390.000 or Completa Gs. 690.000. Confetti balloon, sign and optional decor. Record the customer’s chosen reminder month, not medical data.
2. Baby Shower: estimates from Gs. 1.150.000 / 1.850.000 / 2.750.000. Main margin event.
3. Primer Añito: estimate from Gs. 1.950.000. Theme-driven (Dino, Moana, Minnie, Mickey). KWP confirms demand. Count it as probability-weighted (assume 25% repeat), not as summed package prices.
4. Optional between 2 and 3: Bautismo, sesión de fotos newborn (referral commission only).

**Booking-mode risk controls (Gate C; not inquiry launch blockers):**
- Never take a seña before the supplier has confirmed the date in writing (WhatsApp screenshot into CRM).
- Two suppliers minimum for decor, one backup for mesa dulce, before ads exceed 1M PYG/month.
- Supplier never gets the customer's phone number. We are on site or the supplier's staff wear no own-brand material at the event (put in the agreement).
- Seña 50% at booking, saldo 48 horas antes del evento. Cancellation: no flat "seña no reembolsable". Itemised: 15 days or more full refund minus documented committed costs; 4–14 days refund minus committed catering/materials shown line by line; under 4 days larger committed costs, itemised. One free date change with 7 days notice if capacity allows. Supplier failure: replacement of equal spec or full refund of the undelivered part, we remedy the customer first. Ley 4868 (comercio electrónico) has retract rights: a Paraguayan lawyer reviews the terms before the first seña. Refund processing target: dentro de 5 días hábiles; lawyer review includes weather and scope changes. The planned policy appears only on /como-funciona/ and /terminos/ under Política prevista de reserva (se aplica cuando habilitemos reservas), using `04-CONTENT-AND-COPY.md` §1.
- Invoicing and payment rails are Gate C decisions (Q1/Q2). POLICY.iva: Estimaciones orientativas. Impuestos y formas de pago se confirman por escrito antes de cualquier pago. POLICY.pagos: Te confirmamos las formas de pago disponibles por WhatsApp antes de cualquier pago. No tax or rail certainty before confirmation.
- Character themes (Moana, Minnie, Mickey, Blanca Nieves, Frutillita, Jefe en Pañales): local decorators all use them, but the site never claims licensed products. Copy says "inspirada en", imagery never depicts the characters, and the safe defaults (Dino, Safari, Mariposas, Nubes y ositos) are pushed first in Ads.
- Reveal add-ons: humo de color and polvo holi are Adicionales a confirmar, solo exterior, without price or calculator entry, never the default. Default reveal mechanism is the confetti balloon or the cake (contained, indoor-safe).
- Reseller ops rule: every fulfilled event yields three authorised photos, an actual-vs-budget margin line, and a neutral review request (Codex idea #7). Lost-sale reasons are logged in the CRM (unavailable date, zone, budget gap, theme) and reviewed monthly (Codex idea #9).
